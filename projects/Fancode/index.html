FanCode: Unlocking Mega-Event Monetization

A product management case study on why FanCode loses revenue during high-stakes sports events, and how to fix payments, restore trust and then grow revenue per match.

The Problem

FanCode's promise is to pay only for what you watch. But during mega-events, high-intent users drop off at checkout and revenue is limited to match passes.

  • Payment friction: UPI failures, OTP delays and app freezes right before kickoff
  • No supplementary revenue: no add-ons or bundles to earn more from an engaged user

What 30+ Users Told Us

  • Payment friction is the number one revenue blocker
  • Users expect about Rs 15–30 for regular matches and Rs 30–59 for high-stakes events
  • Ads on paid content cause the most anger
  • Streaming reliability is a constant complaint
  • Users will pay more for premium experiences: ad-free, F1 features, regional commentary
  • JioCinema and Hotstar are seen as smoother and cheaper

Personas

F1 Aficionado

High ARPU, low price sensitivity. Annoyed by ads on a paid season pass and confusion over pass validity.

Opportunity: F1 add-ons, loyalty bundles

Football Loyalist

Mid ARPU, price sensitive. Hit by wrong pass activation, freezing and lag.

Opportunity: matchday bundles, regional commentary

Cricket Value Seeker

High volume, very price sensitive. Faces payment failures during India matches and ads after paying.

Opportunity: micro-passes, dynamic pricing

Solution: Fix First, Then Improve

Layer 1: Fix what's broken

  • One-tap UPI checkout: remember the last payment method
  • Pay → access guarantee: payment succeeds, user watches immediately; reconciliation runs in the background
  • Ad-free for paid users: or say so before checkout
  • Auto refunds for failed streams: instant credit, no support ticket

Layer 2: Improve and grow

  • Event recovery credits: applied automatically after issues
  • Watch-first micro-trial: 60–90 seconds free, then pay
  • Contextual discounts: auto-applied after failures, no coupon codes
  • Kickoff window mode: simplified checkout in the last 5 minutes

Success Metrics

North Star: successful paid live views per mega-event user. A view counts only if payment completes, the pass activates correctly and the user actually watches.

Leading

  • Checkout-to-payment success rate
  • UPI success rate at peak
  • Time to pass activation
  • Stream start success, buffering in first 2 minutes

Lagging

  • Revenue per match
  • Payment completion
  • Repeat purchase rate
  • Mega-event ARPU, season pass adoption

Guardrails

  • Ads shown per paid session
  • Refund SLA under 48 hours
  • Payment disputes per 1,000 users
  • Support tickets and negative reviews per event

Roadmap

Phase 1 · Months 0–3 · Stabilise

One-tap UPI, access guarantee, multi-gateway routing, ad-free paid streams.

Phase 2 · Months 3–6 · Restore trust

Recovery credits, auto refunds with SLA, stream health checks, post-event recovery messages.

Phase 3 · Months 6–12 · Grow revenue

Micro-trial, kickoff window mode, premium F1 pack, matchday and group bundles.

Go-to-Market Principles

  • Never disrupt a live match
  • Ship silently, message deliberately
  • Build trust before pushing upsells
  • Use moments of high intent, not generic announcements
  • Localise messaging by sport and fan type

Phase 1 shadow-launches on ISL first. No generic launch emails, coupon blasts or popups during live matches.
