DreamStreet: First-Time Investor Trust and Activation

A product management case study on why first-time investors reject a new broking app before they try it, and how to fix trust, KYC drop-off and early retention, including the role of its AI assistant, Veda.

Context

About DreamStreet

  • SEBI-registered stock broker from Dream Sports (parent of Dream11), launched May 2026
  • Target user: first-time investors, 22–35, urban India, ₹4–15L income, no demat account yet
  • Stocks and ETFs only at launch, no mutual funds

Market

  • Active retail clients shrinking for the first time in five years (4.57 Cr in FY26)
  • Groww: 1.29 Cr clients, 28% share. Zerodha: 68.9L and declining
  • Real-money gaming ban wiped out about 95% of Dream11 revenue

The Problem

DreamStreet loses first-time investors at the trust layer, not the product layer. More than half of potential users reject the brand before they experience the product.

1. Brand rejection (pre-install)

"Dream11 = gambling app" blocks trust before sign-up.

2. KYC trust breaks

PAN pre-filled with no explanation, an unknown "Digio" on the consent screen, and "Finverse Platform Pvt Ltd" on the eSign document, never disclosed earlier.

3. Activation with no guide

A 1–2 day mandatory wait with no engagement plan, then a home screen of speculative stock tips and no ETF starter path.

India-specific insights

  • Trust is family-mediated: first-timers must convince a spouse or parents
  • Dream11 carries a negative association in public memory, not a neutral one
  • Experienced investors are harder to convert; first-timers are the real opportunity
  • Mutual funds and SIPs are the default "start investing" model, so ETFs need positioning as a bridge

Solutions

S1 · Trust pre-screen

One screen before account opening: SEBI number, "your money is separate from Dream Sports", accounts opened so far, and a 15-second real-user testimonial.

S2 · SEBI badge everywhere

SEBI registration and "SEBI-regulated broker" in the app store listing, splash screen and above the fold on the homepage.

S3 · Explanation copy at three screens

One reassuring sentence before PAN pre-fill, the Digio redirect and the eSign document.

S4 · Day-1 portfolio push

A 9:30 AM notification the morning after the first investment showing how it is doing.

S5 · Veda "Explain before you invest"

On first visit to the investment screen, Veda offers an optional 2-minute explainer on what a Nifty 50 ETF is.

S6 · Veda "Gyaan Path"

A 7-day, ~90-second-a-day learning sequence from ETF basics to tax basics and setting a SIP-style goal.

S7 · Referral with a portfolio card

Share a masked-balance portfolio card with the SEBI badge, not a generic link. The referred friend gets ₹100 invested on sign-up.

Veda, the AI Assistant

What Veda can do

  • Explain concepts in plain Hindi or English
  • Explain market moves ("your portfolio is down ₹47 today, here's why")
  • Answer "is this safe?" using SEBI framework facts
  • Run the 7-day Gyaan Path and the optional ETF explainer

What Veda cannot do (SEBI rules)

  • Recommend specific stocks
  • Time the market or suggest sectors
  • Tell users to sell a holding
  • Give portfolio allocation advice

In the survey, 7 of 18 said Veda not recommending stocks is better. The regulatory limit is positioned as a trust feature.

North Star and Metrics

D14 Active Investor Rate: the share of app installs that make at least one investment and return within 14 days. Baseline about 6%, target above 15% in 90 days.

Leading

  • App store install conversion
  • KYC drop-off per step
  • Day-1 notification open rate
  • Account activation return rate

Lagging

  • KYC to first investment rate
  • D14 Active Investor Rate
  • NPS after first investment

Go-to-Market Roadmap

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2 · Trust foundation

SEBI badge, trust pre-screen, brand separation page, upfront disclosure of MF status, legal entity and F&O.

Phase 2 · Weeks 3–6 · Funnel conversion

Reassurance copy at PAN, Digio and eSign; activation engagement plan; Day-1 notification; Veda explainer.

Phase 3 · Weeks 6–10 · First investment

ETF Starter Pack, KYC re-engagement nudge, Veda 7-day Gyaan Path.

Phase 4 · Week 10+ · Growth loop

Read-only family portfolio view, referral with portfolio card, ₹100 invested for referred friends.

Takeaway

The product is already compliant and competitive. It just doesn't communicate it. Fix trust first, then bring users in.
