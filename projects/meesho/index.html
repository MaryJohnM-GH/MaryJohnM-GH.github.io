Meesho: Increasing Transactions per User

A product thinking case study on why Meesho has strong reach but low purchase frequency, and which fixes to prioritise using user research, personas and RICE scoring.

The Problem

Meesho reaches a large user base, but users buy less often and keep cart values low. The objective was to increase transactions per user (a lever on ARPU) by improving trust, experience and reliability.

  • Buy less frequently than on competing platforms
  • Deliberately keep cart values low because of perceived risk
  • Compare Meesho with Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra before buying

Group exercise with Shubham Pawar and Monika Jilla. Based on survey responses, personas, Meesho's annual report and public articles.

User Research

Approach

  • Google Form survey with demographic and behavioural questions
  • 21 responses from surveys and 1:1 phone conversations
  • Competitive comparison across Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra/Ajio

Key Insights

  • Trust and product quality are the top barriers to frequent purchases
  • Delivery problems and slow support hurt repeat usage
  • Many users keep carts small to limit risk

Personas

Tushar: Value-first professional

25–34, Tier 1/2. Buys home/kitchen items and men's wear. Worried about authenticity, delivery delays and limited brands.

Pooja: Modern homemaker

25–44, Tier 1. Buys women's wear and home items. Frustrated by inconsistent quality and a slow app; prefers Myntra.

Sheetal: Value-oriented deal seeker

45+, Tier 2, professional. Medium cart value, actively hunts deals, high potential for cart value growth.

Saniya: Impulse buyer

18–34, Tier 1, professional. Quick decisions, little time in app; held back by trust, delivery and weak support.

Prioritisation (RICE)

Ideas were first grouped on an impact-effort matrix, then scored with a directional RICE model. With no internal data, scores are relative, not exact.

Key Learning

Trust and delivery experience move transactions per user more than adding premium brands.

Quick wins in customer support and transparency pay off faster than a major platform overhaul.
