VitaFit Engagement Boost

A product management case study focused on improving fitness app retention through personalized rewards and community-driven engagement.

The Problem

VitaFit users showed strong initial engagement but many dropped off after the first week. Existing gamification mechanisms such as leaderboards did not appeal to all users and failed to support different motivational styles.

  • Loss of motivation after initial onboarding
  • Limited sense of community
  • Weak instructor connection
  • One-size-fits-all engagement model

Solution Overview

🎯 Personalized Rewards

  • Motivation-style onboarding quiz
  • Private milestones
  • Adaptive rewards
  • Optional streaks
  • Personalized progression system

🤝 Community Challenges

  • Instructor-led challenges
  • Solo participation options
  • Small-group challenges
  • Community interaction
  • Optional leaderboards
